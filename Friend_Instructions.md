# IST 102A Individual Assignment – what to do with the Submission folder

The `Submission` folder has everything needed for both exercises. **No name is on any file**, so add yours before sending.

## 1. Add your name
- **`BrightHome_Reflection.docx`**: replace `[Student Name]` and `[Reg No]` on the second line with your own details, then save.
- **`GreenGrid_Energy_Report.xlsx`**: open it, go to the **Interpretation** sheet, type your name and reg number in cell **A2** (under the heading), then save.
- If your lecturer wants the files named with your details, rename them (e.g. `YourRegNo_GreenGrid.xlsx`, `YourRegNo_BrightHome.accdb`) but keep the extensions.

## 2. Quick check before sending (2 minutes)
- **Excel file:** open it. You should see 4 sheets (Data, Assumptions, Summary, Interpretation). The Summary sheet has two charts, and Data has a green-to-red colour scale on Total Energy with the April and November rows highlighted red.
- **Access file (`BrightHome.accdb`):** double-click to open. If you see a yellow *Security Warning* bar, click **Enable Content**. In the left pane you should see 5 tables, queries Q1–Q5, a form `frmJobs` and a report `rptJobsByTechnician`.
  - Opening **Q2_CustomerJobs** asks for a customer name. Type e.g. `Amina Wanjiru` to test it.
- **`Relationships.png`:** open it. It shows the 5 tables linked together.
- Close Access completely before copying the `.accdb` (otherwise a lock file `BrightHome.laccdb` appears next to it; do not send that file).

## 3. Send
Send these four files only:
1. `GreenGrid_Energy_Report.xlsx`
2. `BrightHome.accdb`
3. `Relationships.png`
4. `BrightHome_Reflection.docx`

The assignment is due **18 November 2026**. Submit to whoever or wherever your lecturer specified.

## Optional: if your lecturer wants it in your own words
The reflection (Exercise 2) and the interpretation paragraph (Excel, Interpretation sheet) are written in plain language, but you can reword them so they sound like you. Keep the numbers as they are.
